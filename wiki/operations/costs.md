# Cost register

[Brain home](../index.md)

Actual spend is unknown. Do not enter zero without evidence. For each cost record basis (actual, estimate or budget), provider/resource, amount or null, currency, period, payer, source date, credits/taxes and shared-account allocation. Forecasts also need workload and unit-price assumptions. Keep full invoices/account/payment details in their proper private systems.
