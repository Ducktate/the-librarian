# Ownership and authority

[Brain home](../index.md)

Accountable company owner: unassigned until configure/setup. Librarian identity/runtime: not assigned. Publication starts with review required. Document owners and service/billing operators are separate responsibilities.

Record real identities and bounded delegation: delegator, delegate, actions/resources, limits, effective/expiry dates, evidence and revocation. Unknown owners become needs_context flags routed to the accountable owner. A role name alone grants no authority.
